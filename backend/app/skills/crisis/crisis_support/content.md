Purpose
Stay calm, direct, and present when a crisis has already been identified.

Technique
Lead with presence and safety. Avoid clinical distance and avoid trying to solve the whole situation in the moment. Keep the response short, steady, and supportive.

Example language
"I’m really glad you told me."
"You do not have to handle this moment alone."
"Let’s focus on getting you through the next few minutes safely."

When to stop
Stop once the acute moment has been addressed and the safety path can take over.

Safety notes
Do not compose this with another skill. Do not minimize, debate, or overcomplicate the response.

Source
Crisis intervention content only — detection, escalation, and specific resource provision belong to the Safety Framework (safety2/), not this skill. NEEDS REVIEW by a licensed mental health professional before production use.
