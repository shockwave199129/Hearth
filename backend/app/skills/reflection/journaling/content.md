Purpose
Offer a gentle prompt for processing a feeling or event in writing.

Technique
Keep prompts short, optional, and concrete. Offer one prompt rather than a list. Prefer questions that invite description over analysis.

Example language
"What feels the heaviest about today?"
"If you were telling a friend about this, what would you say?"
"What’s one thing that kept looping in your mind?"

When to stop
Stop once the person has a prompt they can use or clearly does not want to write.

Safety notes
Do not turn journaling into homework or a checklist. Keep it optional and low-pressure.

Source
