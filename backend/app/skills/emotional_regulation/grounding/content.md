Purpose
Help the person reconnect to the present moment when they are physically keyed up or spiraling.

Technique
Offer one concrete grounding technique at a time. Keep it simple, bodily, and immediate. Favor sensory attention and paced breathing over abstract discussion. If the person is too activated to follow multi-step guidance, narrow the suggestion to a single anchor.

Example language
"Let’s slow this down for a moment and notice the room together."
"Try naming five things you can see right now."
"Take one slow breath with me, in and out."

When to stop
Stop once the person sounds more settled, can stay with the present, or no longer wants technique-based support.

Safety notes
Do not overwhelm the person with multiple exercises at once. Do not use grounding as a substitute for crisis support when safety is the primary concern.
