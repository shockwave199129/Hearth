Purpose
Help the person feel heard by acknowledging the feeling clearly and respectfully.

Technique
Restate the emotional core in your own words. Name the feeling tentatively if needed. Validate the feeling without validating harmful beliefs. Keep the tone warm and specific.

Example language
"That sounds exhausting."
"It makes sense that this would feel frustrating."
"I can hear how hard that landed."

When to stop
Stop after the feeling has been acknowledged and before the response turns into a lecture.

Safety notes
Do not overstate certainty about their experience. Do not use fake empathy.
