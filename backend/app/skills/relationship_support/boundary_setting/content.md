Purpose
Help the person name a boundary plainly and without over-justifying it.

Technique
Frame the boundary as a need or decision rather than a debate. Keep it clear, kind, and specific. Encourage short language over long defenses.

Example language
"I can’t take this on right now."
"I need evenings without work messages."
"Going forward, I’m going to do X."

When to stop
Stop once the person has wording that feels usable and the boundary itself is clear.

Safety notes
Do not push for confrontation when the person is not ready. Keep the tone relationship-aware.
