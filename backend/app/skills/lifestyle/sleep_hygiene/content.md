Purpose
Offer one or two practical habits that help the person wind down for sleep.

Technique
Focus on wake time, wind-down, and reducing stimulation before bed. Keep suggestions concrete and small. If thoughts are racing, pair a short brain dump with one calming routine.

Example language
"A steady wake-up time tends to matter more than forcing a perfect bedtime."
"If your mind keeps spinning, writing the thoughts down can help them loosen a bit."
"A short wind-down routine can do more than one big fix."

When to stop
Stop once the person has a usable next step or the conversation naturally moves on.

Safety notes
Do not imply a sleep habit tweak can solve persistent insomnia on its own.
